Redis blob storage keeps its blobs under the ``harp:blob:`` key prefix: the janitor sweep and the metrics no longer list, delete or fail on keys of other applications sharing the Redis database. Blobs stored before the change are still read and deleted by id, but never listed: the janitor does not sweep them and the HTTP cache does not expire their entries (``cache/meta``) by TTL, see :ref:`upgrading-0-13`.

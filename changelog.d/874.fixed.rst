A response carrying only an ``ETag`` or ``Last-Modified``, with no ``Cache-Control`` or ``Expires``, is retained again and revalidated on each use: a ``304`` serves the stored body instead of a full refetch, and it is never served without the origin confirming it (``allow_heuristics`` still opts into heuristic freshness). Limits: a response with ``Cache-Control: no-cache`` is still not retained, and ``allow_stale: true`` serves such a response stale like any other.

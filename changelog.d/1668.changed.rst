Applications now declare their periodic cleanups as sweeps (name, interval, budget, function) in the core, and the janitor schedules and measures them without knowing any application. Storage declares the transaction purge, the orphan blob purge and the metrics pass, and startup warns when sweeps are declared but no janitor is loaded. The transaction retention is now the `storage.retention_days` setting; `janitor.old_after` still works as a deprecated alias with a warning, and the janitor no longer loads storage. Audit retention is declared as the `audit.retention` sweep and no longer appended to the janitor.

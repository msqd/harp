⚠️ Response bodies are streamed to the client as they arrive. HARP records at most ``proxy.max_recorded_body_size`` (1 MiB) of each, measured decoded: a longer body is recorded truncated, marked ``truncated-response-body`` and not stored in the HTTP cache. A client leaving mid-body is marked ``client-disconnected``. Transactions end once the body is sent, so their duration and TPDEX now cover the transfer to the client.

Transactions record their cache outcome (hit, stale, revalidated, miss, uncacheable) with the age, the freshness left, the cache key, the variant and the invalidation number. The transaction detail shows them and the transaction list can be filtered by outcome. Limits: a response stored after its headers went out counts as a miss even if the store then refuses it, a response that replaced a stale one counts as a miss, and transactions recorded before the upgrade carry no outcome, key, variant or number.
